# AOF v10.7.1 Release Notes

## What You Can Do Now

You can trust `capability-coverage-audit` to reject completed work when the actor output chain is incomplete.

Before v10.7.1, AOF could prove that required actors were assigned and gated, but still miss that completed work lacked schema-valid Role Result, Team Output, or Council Review linkage.

After v10.7.1, AOF verifies that required actors produced role results, the team joined those results, and Council reviewed the actual outputs before the work can count as capability-covered.

## Capability Delta

UPDATED:

- PCAP-CAPABILITY-COVERAGE-GATE: now checks packet-to-evaluation-to-gate linkage, done-work role results, team output aggregation, Council review schema, and Japanese follow-up risk language.

NEW:

- None. This is a patch release that strengthens the v10.7 capability rather than adding a new product surface.

- None.

## Value Evidence

- `capability-coverage-audit` now fails when actor skill packets are not matched to their own selected evaluation and allowed execution gate.
- `capability-coverage-audit` now fails when done work lacks role results for required roles.
- `capability-coverage-audit` now fails when team output is missing, schema-invalid, not ready for Council review, or omits required role outputs.
- `capability-coverage-audit` now fails when Council review does not include the role results and team output it judged.
- Regression tests cover unmatched linkage, missing output chains, and Japanese Council risk notes without follow-up tasks.

## What Changed

v10.7.1 is a patch release for the v10.7 Capability Coverage Gate.

It fixes an audit blind spot: v10.7.0 could pass capability coverage while the completed work output chain was not fully validated.

## Capability Impact

Operators can now rely on `capability-coverage-audit` to check the complete handoff chain for scoped done work:

- each actor skill packet must have its own selected assignment evaluation
- each actor skill packet must have its own allowed execution gate
- completed work must include role result artifacts for required roles
- role results must be schema-valid and completed
- team output must be schema-valid, ready for council review, and include all required role outputs
- council review must be schema-valid and include the role results and team output it judged
- Japanese Council risk notes such as `不足`, `未検証`, `追加検証`, and `フォローアップ` require preserved follow-up task references
