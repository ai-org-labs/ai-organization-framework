# AOF v11.6.0 Release Notes

## What You Can Do Now

AOF can now treat external operator comprehension as product evidence. An operator can inspect one feedback record and see:

- whether the replay was understood
- whether the replay was reproduced
- what questions or confusing points remained
- whether the feedback should be accepted or routed back into product review
- what remains unproven

## Capability Delta

NEW:

- External Operator Reproduction Feedback Intake

UPDATED:

- Human-readable Provider Observation Replay now has a feedback intake gate.
- Product Value Comprehension now has a stronger route for "the operator did not understand this."

REMOVED:

- None.

## Product Benchmark

Before:

- AOF could generate a provider observation replay, but a reviewer still had to decide outside the runtime whether the replay was understandable or reproducible.

After:

- AOF records understanding, reproduction, questions, blockers, and governance route as a schema-validated artifact. Weak feedback must trigger product review instead of being hidden behind a green release claim.

Five-minute demo:

- Run `node ./src/cli.js external-operator-feedback-audit --project .`
- Open `.aof/artifacts/external-operator-feedback/EOF-TASK-137-V116.json`
- Confirm the record states understanding result, reproduction result, feedback summary, confusing points, governance route, product evidence value, and not-proven boundary.
